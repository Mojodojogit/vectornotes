Three steps and you’re in

One form for both platforms. Pick Android, Windows or both.

Android Tablet and phone Beta

  1. Sign up in the form with the e-mail address of your Google account.
  2. You get an e-mail with the beta links right away.
  3. Tap “Become a tester” on Google Play and install the app.

Google sometimes needs a few minutes before the test link works for you.

Open the form

Windows PC, laptop and tablet Beta

  1. In the same form, tick Windows and enter your personal Microsoft account (e.g. @outlook.com or @hotmail.com).
  2. We unlock the account in the Microsoft Store, usually within a day.
  3. Open the Store page while signed in with that account and install. Updates arrive automatically.

Work and school accounts unfortunately don’t work in the Microsoft Store.

One request: stay for 14 days

Google only releases a new app to everyone once enough testers have kept it installed for 14 days in a row. So please keep the app for at least two weeks, even if you don’t use it daily. That alone helps a lot.

Your feedback comes straight to us

The app menu has a Feedback entry: wish, bug or anything else, one sentence is enough. Every message gets read, and a lot of the beta feedback is already in the app.

Questions

Does the beta cost anything?

No. The beta is free, with no ads and no subscription.

Do I need an account with you?

No. We only need your Google or Microsoft account address so the store can unlock the app for you. The app itself needs no account.

Where are my notes stored?

On your device. If you want, the app syncs them through your own Google Drive or OneDrive. Details are in the privacy policy.

Which devices work?

Android tablets and phones, and Windows PCs, laptops and tablets. Best with a pen, but finger, mouse and keyboard work too.

Can I leave again?

Yes, any time: uninstall the app and choose “Leave the test” on Google Play.
