AI assistant · AI correction

AI, when you want it.

The AI assistant explains, checks and solves what you select. AI correction improves the recognized text of your handwriting. Both with your own API key – your data only goes to the provider you choose.

Your choice of provider
Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT) or xAI (Grok). Gemini has a free tier.
Only what you send
Only the selected area is sent – directly to the provider, not through Vector Notes servers.
Your key stays with you
Stored encrypted on the device in the apps, never in cloud sync. Without a key, no request goes out.

Thought through to the details

New features from the current version of the app.

Sidebar and quick switch

The sidebar shows folders and documents; a swipe from the edge brings up your recently used notes as thumbnails. One tap – and you are in the next document.

Just ask: “Where do I find …?”

The feature search understands your own words, shows the way through the menus and, if you like, the exact spot – entirely offline.

Move from other apps

Export notes from Samsung Notes and other apps as PDF, then import whole folders or many files at once. Handwriting from PDF annotations becomes editable; the cover eraser removes writing from other exports.

Paper with character

Page colors from white through sepia to slate and paper textures like recycled or watercolor – for imported documents and blank pages.

Tools that adapt

Frequent settings sit at the top of the tool panel. The toolbar scales with the display: full on tablets, compact on phones – adjustable under “Interface size”.

What Vector Notes can do

Built for the pen: everything you need to write on a tablet as freely as on paper.

Write with your pen

Fountain pen, ballpoint, brush, pencil, fineliner, calligraphy, marker and highlighter – pressure-sensitive, with your own pens and adjustable smoothing.

Live: handwriting to text

Just write and Vector Notes turns it into text – on the device, in English and German. If you like, AI correction improves the result.

PDF, Word and PowerPoint

Insert documents as pages and mark them up with your pen. Copy text from PDFs, cut out regions, offline text recognition.

Cover eraser

Erase text, images or handwriting in imported documents. The original stays underneath.

Lasso and shapes

Select, move, rotate, recolor. Lines, arrows, circles and coordinate systems become clean shapes when you hold.

Designs and backgrounds

Light and dark, drafting table, Sumi-e, Cyberpunk; backgrounds like paper, linen, wood or slate – or your own photo.

OneDrive, Google Drive, Moodle

Optional sync through your own OneDrive or Google Drive. Open files from Moodle, Nextcloud and WebDAV directly.

Lock a document

Fix a document to page width: scroll vertically only, nothing shifts while you write. Zoom and rotation can be locked.

Print and share

Print frames and preview for A4 pages, export as PDF or image.
